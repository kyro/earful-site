Talk to all your bots.

Just say your bot’s name. Earful sends your words straight there.

Works with the AI bots you already use.

  • Tap to talk

    Start with a bot’s name, and your note goes to that bot. With no name, it goes to your main bot. Talk from a widget, the Action Button or your Index 01 ring too.

  • Your bots, your way

    Connect the bots you use. Earful doesn’t make new ones. If a bot can take a webhook, Earful can send it notes.

  • Private by design

    Recordings never leave your iPhone. Your words go only to the bot you name, and to your iPhone’s own backups. There’s no Earful account and no Earful server.
